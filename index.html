Knowledge Base

What is ICE?

Integrated Client Engine — A complete system to acquire high-ticket clients without manual grinding. Three engines work together: ASP (Vehicle), Traffic+Trust (Fuel), ICE (Machine).

Engine Flow

1. Traffic → Watch AI Twin (ASP)
2. High-Intent → Apply
3. Auto-Qualify → ICE Nurture (Qualified) or ProofLoop (Unqualified)
4. Pre-Sold → Call → Close
5. Automated Payment Collection

Why Hinglish?

India-first approach. Natural language for ICP who thinks: "Pipeline unpredictable hai", "Selling nahi aati". Creates instant relatability and trust.
